Built so your file is never trapped in one building.

Most record systems stop at the hospital's front door. When you switch clinics, get referred to a specialist, or need care while travelling, your history usually has to be requested, faxed, or re-entered by hand. PDTS keeps one authoritative copy of your record and gives every facility you authorise a live view of it.

You decide who sees it Every facility needs your explicit authorisation before viewing or adding to your file.
Updates in real time New results and prescriptions appear in your file within minutes of being recorded.
One number, one history No duplicate records across facilities, every provider works from the same file.

What the system handles for you

Four things every facility, patient, and referring physician relies on when a record needs to move.

Instant record retrieval

Pull a patient's full history, imaging, and lab results in seconds instead of waiting on a records request between facilities.

Encryption by design

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, with access logging built in so you can review activity on your record.

Cross-facility syncing

Referrals, discharge notes, and prescriptions written at one facility are visible to the next one automatically, once authorised.

24/7 access

View your own file, appointment history, and prescriptions from a phone or computer any time, not just during clinic hours.
